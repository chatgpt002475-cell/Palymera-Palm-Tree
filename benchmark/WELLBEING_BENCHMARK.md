## 2. Product Architecture & Catalog Benchmarking

From scraping and indexing `wellbeingorganic.com`, the core offering spans three verticals:

### Vertical A: Low-GI Heritage Sweeteners
1. **Crystallized Palm Jaggery (Palm Sugar)**
   - *Benchmark Features:* Granulated free-flowing form, rich caramel earthy taste, low glycemic index (~35-40).
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* Retains traditional aroma without scorched off-notes; available in 250g, 500g, and 1kg eco-pouches.
2. **Palm Sugar Crystals (Panakarkandu / Tal Mishri)**
   - *Benchmark Features:* Slowly evaporated palm sap crystals; traditional Ayurvedic remedy for sore throats, coughs, and infant digestion.
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* Sun-matured, unbleached golden-amber crystals; free from chemical clarifiers (no sulfur / hydros).
3. **Dry Ginger & Pepper Palm Jaggery (Sukku Karupatti)**
   - *Benchmark Features:* Infused with dried ginger, black pepper, and coriander seeds.
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* Authentic decoction-infused blocks and granular shakers for instant Ayurvedic bedtime milk or morning brew.
4. **Unrefined Coconut Palm Sugar**
   - *Benchmark Features:* High solubility, delicate floral caramel notes, mineral-rich.
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* Sustainable companion sweetener for baking, desserts, and lattes.

### Vertical B: Ancient Millets & Prebiotic Superfoods
1. **Finger Millet Flakes (Ragi Aval / Poha)**
   - *Benchmark Features:* Ready-to-cook steamed and pressed flakes, rich in calcium and dietary fiber.
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* Whole-grain breakfast flakes pairing with warm palm milk and fruits.
2. **Multi-Millet Flakes**
   - *Benchmark Features:* Blend of Foxtail, Little, Kodo, and Pearl millets for balanced micronutrients.
   - *Palmyra Palm Implementation:* High-satiety, gut-friendly breakfast base.
3. **Palmyra Sprout Prebiotic Flour (Panai Kizhangu)**
   - *Exclusive Addition:* Rare traditional delicacy made from underground sprouted palm seeds, packed with resistant starch and gut-healing fiber.

### Vertical C: Zero-Waste Eco-Living
- **Handwoven Palmyra Leaf Baskets & Hampers**: Honoring the palm tree’s 801 documented traditional uses; replacing single-use plastic packaging.

## 3. Glycemic Index (GI) & Nutritional Comparison

Wellbeing Organic places Glycemic Index at the center of its brand narrative. We incorporate their clinical data into an interactive visual comparison matrix:

| Sweetener | Glycemic Index (GI) | Fructose / Glucose Ratio | Iron (mg/100g) | Potassium (mg/100g) | Magnesium (mg/100g) | Refining / Bleaching |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Palmyra Palm Jaggery** | **35 - 40 (Low)** | Balanced Natural Sucrose + Complex Polyols | **11.2 mg** | **1,050 mg** | **70 mg** | **Zero (Raw Evaporated Sap)** |
| **Palm Sugar Crystals (Panakarkandu)** | **35 (Low)** | Slow-crystallized natural disaccharides | **8.4 mg** | **820 mg** | **45 mg** | **Zero (Sun/Vat crystallized)** |
| **White Refined Sugar** | **65 - 70 (High)** | 50% Fructose / 50% Glucose | 0.05 mg | 2 mg | 0.1 mg | Heavy (Sulfur, Bone Char, Lime) |
| **Standard Cane Jaggery** | **55 - 60 (Medium)** | Rapidly absorbed sugars | 3.5 mg | 300 mg | 25 mg | Moderate to High clarifying agents |
| **Commercial Honey** | **58 - 62 (Medium)** | High Free Fructose (fructose overload) | 0.5 mg | 52 mg | 2 mg | Thermal pasteurization & micro-filtering |
| **Agave Nectar** | **15 - 20 (Low)** | High Free Fructose (up to 85% - liver strain) | 0.1 mg | 4 mg | 1 mg | Heavy industrial enzymatic processing |

### Key Scientific Takeaways Implemented:
- **No Insulin Rollercoaster:** High GI sweeteners trigger sharp blood glucose surges followed by reactionary reactive hypoglycemia (the dreaded 3 PM crash). Low-GI Palmyra palm sweetener provides sustained glucose delivery over 3-4 hours.
- **Rich in Trace Minerals:** Natural source of zinc, iron, copper, and plant-derived Vitamin B12 rarely found in plant saps.
